The Legacy of TechCon: Pioneering the Future of Technology

Founded in 2012 in Silicon Valley, TechCon began as a 150-person gathering of tech pioneers from Google, MIT, and startups, focused on the mobile revolution. Held at Stanford, it featured keynotes, workshops, and hackathons, sparking three startup launches. By 2015, it expanded globally to London and Singapore, embracing IoT and AI. In 2017, TechCon launched the Innovation Awards, honoring breakthroughs like a solar-powered edge device, and boosted diversity, raising female and minority participation from 20% to 45%. The 2020 pandemic prompted a virtual pivot, reaching 25,000 attendees with AR networking and AI Q&As. Returning in-person in 2022 at Moscone Center, TechCon adopted sustainable practices, including carbon-neutral badges. Now hosting 10,000+ attendees from 50+ countries, TechCon 2026 will explore quantum computing, ethical AI, and climate tech, continuing 13 years of fostering 500+ collaborations and countless innovations.

Key Milestones:

  • 2005: Launched in Silicon Valley, 150 attendees.
  • 2015: Expanded to London, Singapore.
  • 2017: Innovation Awards and diversity initiatives introduced.
  • 2020: Virtual format, 25,000 global attendees.
  • 2022: In-person return with sustainability focus.
  • 2024: Upcoming focus on AI ethics, quantum tech.

TechCon Mission Statement

TechCon's mission is to ignite innovation and shape the future of technology by uniting diverse minds—engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, and visionaries—in a collaborative platform. We are dedicated to bridging cutting-edge research with real-world impact, fostering groundbreaking ideas in fields like AI, quantum computing, and sustainable tech. Through dynamic keynotes, hands-on workshops, and inclusive initiatives, we empower a global community to tackle pressing challenges, drive ethical advancements, and create solutions that benefit humanity. Committed to sustainability and diversity, TechCon cultivates a movement where today's ideas spark tomorrow's transformative realities.

Past Speakers

Fei-Fei Li

Fei-Fei Li is a pioneering AI researcher, co-director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute, and a professor at Stanford University. Born in China and educated at Princeton and Caltech, she has authored over 200 publications and co-founded AI4ALL to promote diversity in AI. Contributions: Known as the "Godmother of AI," Li created ImageNet, a massive visual database that trained deep learning models, catalyzing breakthroughs in computer vision used in self-driving cars, medical imaging, and facial recognition. Her work has influenced global AI ethics discussions and applications in healthcare, impacting millions through accessible tech advancements.

Sundar Pichai

Sundar Pichai is the CEO of Alphabet Inc. and Google, roles he has held since 2015 and 2019, respectively. A graduate of IIT Kharagpur and Stanford, Pichai joined Google in 2004, rising through product management to oversee Chrome, Android, and later the entire company. Contributions: Pichai spearheaded the development of Google Chrome, which revolutionized web browsing with its speed and security, capturing over 65% global market share. Under his leadership, Android became the world's most-used mobile OS, powering billions of devices and democratizing access to information, while initiatives like Google Assistant advanced AI integration in daily life
